About Heimdall

One clearer view of emergency coordination.

Heimdall is presented here as an emergency medical services portal concept inspired by the coordination needs of EMS providers.

✓Clearer design: the existing blue palette, readable text and focused layouts.
✓Four role-aware views: EMS, Nurse, EMS Admin and Hospital Admin.
✓Map access: satellite imagery, sample markers and external navigation.

Independent student design concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any emergency service provider. Not a live emergency service.
