About Heimdall

One clearer view of emergency coordination.

Heimdall is presented here as an emergency medical services portal concept inspired by the coordination needs of EMS providers such as Netcare 911. The goal is to make key information easier to scan for both hospital administrators and ambulance drivers.

✓Clearer design: a consistent blue palette, readable text and focused layouts.
✓Role-aware views: different entry points for hospital and field operations.
✓Map access: a Johannesburg overview with a link to external navigation.

This is an independent student design concept and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netcare 911. It is not a live emergency service.
