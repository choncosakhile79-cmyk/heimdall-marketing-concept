About Heimdall

One clearer view of emergency coordination.

Heimdall is presented here as an emergency medical services portal concept inspired by the coordination needs of EMS providers.

✓Consistent design: existing blue palette and readable layouts retained.
✓Four role-aware views: EMS, Nurse, EMS Admin and Hospital Admin.

This is an independent student design concept. It is not a live emergency service and is not affiliated with any EMS provider.
